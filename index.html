🌱 Who We Are

Global Vision,
Local Action

She Can Foundation is a non-governmental organization registered under the Indian Society Act, 1860, dedicated to uplifting and empowering underprivileged women.

Our mission is to provide education, training, and resources to help women from marginalized communities overcome obstacles and achieve their full potential. We work to promote gender equality, increase access to healthcare and education, and provide opportunities for economic growth.

We work closely with local organizations, governments, and communities to ensure that our programmes are effective and sustainable. Through advocacy campaigns, we raise awareness of women's issues across the globe.

  • Free Sanitary Pads & Awareness Workshops
  • Dignity Kits for Underprivileged Girls
  • Education Support & Resources
  • Healthcare Access Initiatives
  • Advocacy & Gender Equality Campaigns

❤️ The Hard Truth

"Her Period Shouldn't End Her Education"

Every month, a 13-year-old girl in a small village is forced to miss five days of school. Not because she doesn't want to learn — but because she cannot afford something as basic as a sanitary pad.

She hides at home in shame. She uses rags, newspapers, even sand — risking her health and her dignity. Slowly, her dreams of education, freedom, and self-respect begin to fade.

📉

1 in every 5 girls in India drops out of school because of periods.

🚫

Over 12 crore women and girls in India still lack access to proper menstrual hygiene.

✅

She Can Foundation has already helped 1,20,000+ girls with free sanitary pads, awareness workshops, and dignity kits.

"Without you, she waits. With you, she rises."

— She Can Foundation
